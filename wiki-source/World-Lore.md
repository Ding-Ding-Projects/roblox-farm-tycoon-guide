# World Lore

The valley began with a shared arrival hall and empty fields. New farmers chose a plot, placed the first soil, and learned what the land returned when they gave it time.

Wheat paid for the first barn. The barn sheltered animals; the feedmill gave those animals a daily rhythm. The depot, market, bakery, and later workshops grew around the same idea: the town becomes real when its people can use the doors, work the machines, and bring something home.

Older equipment remains in the depot because its makers left practical lessons behind. Every shutter, counter, and room should have a purpose. Ordinary harvests and careful construction shape the town's story.

This is original setting fiction for the public guide. It does not claim that unfinished quests, characters, or buildings are already playable. Read the [complete lore section on the website](https://ding-ding-projects.github.io/roblox-farm-tycoon-guide/#lore).
