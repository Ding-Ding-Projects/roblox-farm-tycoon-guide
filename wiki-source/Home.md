# Farm Tycoon Field Guide

The [live field guide](https://ding-ding-projects.github.io/roblox-farm-tycoon-guide/) has a searchable explanation of every defined crop, seed, item, animal, recipe, building, and upgrade. It also carries the [world lore](https://ding-ding-projects.github.io/roblox-farm-tycoon-guide/#lore) and current beta status. This wiki provides stable reference pages and maintenance notes. The website is documentation, not a playable version of Farm Tycoon.

- [Getting Started](Getting-Started.md)
- [Crops and Items](Crops-and-Items.md)
- [Buildings and Production](Buildings-and-Production.md)
- [World Lore](World-Lore.md)
- [Beta Status](Beta-Status.md)

**Evidence language:** "Observed locally" means a selected route was actually exercised in Studio. "In development" means a definition exists but the full route is unverified. Neither label means that public-server behavior has passed.
