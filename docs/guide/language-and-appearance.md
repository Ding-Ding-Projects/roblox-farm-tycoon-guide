# Guide language and appearance preferences

Home and all seven site-hosted wiki URLs share a settings disclosure immediately after the header. It offers English, playful Hong Kong Cantonese, and bilingual display modes; separate English and Cantonese humor levels from 1 to 5; and light, dark, or device-following appearance. Both humor levels start at 5. The choices are stored per visitor in browser local storage under `farm-tycoon-guide-preferences-v1`. No account or network request is used for these preferences. If storage is unavailable or corrupt, the guide falls back to English, level 5 for each language, and the device appearance. Changing the device color preference updates an open page when appearance follows the device.

The content translation covers the Home explanations and evidence statements, catalogue card facts and notes, all six site-hosted wiki article bodies, search workbench labels and help, image descriptions, and version wording. English remains the source text in `index.html`, `catalogue.js`, and `wiki-source/`; Cantonese counterparts live in `home-yue.js`, `catalogue-yue.js`, and `wiki-source-yue/`. Bilingual mode displays both bodies or both card lines. The two humor controls independently vary a visible note in their respective languages at each level; they do not alter prices, levels, timers, source revisions, or evidence statements. Catalogue and wiki search include Cantonese terms alongside English terms. The chosen appearance applies to the shared guide surfaces, but per-element customization and import/export remain unimplemented.

The wiki browser title and document description follow the selected language after the client script loads. The checked-in HTML title, initial description and social metadata stay in English so a visitor or crawler without JavaScript receives ordinary, accurate source information. The English and Cantonese version dates are formatted independently in their respective locales; both preserve the visitor's current timezone and the same recorded instant.

The settings controls use native selects and range inputs, visible labels, keyboard interaction, and a status message. `node tools/check-preferences.mjs` checks storage validation, independent humor wording, color resolution, all eight HTML shells, and a deliberate missing-route regression. `node tools/check-localization.mjs` checks the exact six-page Cantonese article inventory, translated Home targets, and catalogue title, note, ingredient, and upgrade coverage. Its `--negative` mode removes one page from the checked inventory and must fail. The catalogue and search source checks continue to run. Rendered interaction, narrow layout, assistive technology behavior, and visual parity still require fresh proof from the sanctioned hidden browser route for this candidate.
