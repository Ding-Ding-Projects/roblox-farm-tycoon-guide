Getting Started

Choose a plot, use the owned management board to place starter soil, plant a seed, wait for its current timer, and harvest. Starter soil is free. Owned objects can be rearranged without an extra placement charge, while a new building uses earned materials, coins, its level unlock, and a construction timer.

Wheat is the first crop. Its current timer is five seconds under compact pacing, and one harvest yields three wheat and five XP when storage has room. Three wheat and 40 coins pay for the first barn. Five wheat and 70 coins pay for a feedmill. A free silo has a 30-second legacy timer, compacted to five seconds. The actual entry and interaction paths have only selected local Studio evidence, so published-server behavior must still be checked.

The live guide lists each cost, unlock, timer, product and evidence status. See Crops and Items and Buildings and Production.

開始上手

先揀一塊地並認領，再用自己嘅管理板放起步泥田、播種、等候目前計時器，最後收成。起步泥田免費。已擁有嘅物件可以重新擺位，唔使再畀一次放置費；新建築則要達到解鎖等級，並付出賺返嚟嘅材料、金幣，同等候建造計時器。

小麥係第一種農作物。按目前壓縮節奏，生長計時係五秒；倉庫有位時，每次收成會得到三份小麥同五點經驗值。第一間穀倉要三份小麥同 40 金幣；飼料廠要五份小麥同 70 金幣。免費筒倉原有 30 秒計時，壓縮後係五秒。實際入內同操作流程暫時只有選定嘅本機 Studio 證據，公開伺服器行為仍要再驗證。
