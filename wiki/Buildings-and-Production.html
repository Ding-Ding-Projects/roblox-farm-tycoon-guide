Buildings and Production

The 14 construction definitions are silo, barn, personal market, supplies shop, restaurant, depot, hotel, restroom, feedmill, bakery, dairy, sugar mill, loom and preserves workshop. Every construction card in the live guide gives its level, exact coin and material cost, current timer and role. The free silo, wheat-funded barn and feedmill have selected local Play routes. A complete 14-building concurrent layout and all earned rotations remain unverified.

The feedmill makes four kinds of animal feed. The bakery makes bread, carrot cake and truffle tart; the dairy makes butter and cheese; the sugar mill makes sugar; the loom makes wool cloth; the preserves workshop makes strawberry jam and apple preserves. Each of the 13 recipe cards lists the input quantity, output quantity, station, level and current timer. Definitions are separate from published production proof.

The four upgrade definitions are barn capacity, silo capacity, production queue and land. The current land rule increases the animal placement allowance by one per tier, subject to a cap of 16; it does not increase the construction limit. The guide records each maximum tier, cost formula and capacity effect. A recipe's listed unlock level can precede the level needed to grow an ingredient or build its station: chicken feed lists level 1 but self-grown corn requires level 2, and butter lists level 6 but the owned dairy requires level 8. Purchases are server-authoritative; an incomplete request or unavailable saved profile must not be shown as a successful upgrade.

建築同生產

14 種建築定義包括筒倉、穀倉、私人市場、用品店、餐廳、貨運站、酒店、洗手間、飼料廠、麵包店、乳製品工場、糖廠、織布機同果醬工場。網上指南每張建築卡都列出等級、準確金幣同材料成本、目前計時器，以及用途。免費筒倉、用小麥換取嘅穀倉同飼料廠有選定嘅本機操作路線。全部 14 棟同時擺放，以及所有賺取後旋轉擺放路線，仍未驗證。

飼料廠製作四種動物飼料。麵包店製作麵包、紅蘿蔔蛋糕同松露撻；乳製品工場製作牛油同芝士；糖廠製糖；織布機織羊毛布；果醬工場製作士多啤梨果醬同蘋果蜜餞。13 張食譜卡逐張列出原料數量、產出數量、工作站、等級同目前計時器。資料定義同公開伺服器上嘅生產證明係兩回事。

四種升級定義係穀倉容量、筒倉容量、生產佇列同土地。目前土地規則每升一級增加一個動物擺放名額，上限係 16；唔會增加建築名額。指南列出各項最高級數、成本公式同容量效果。食譜列出嘅解鎖等級，可能早過自己種到原料或者建到工作站嘅等級：雞飼料列等級 1，但自己種粟米要等級 2；牛油列等級 6，但自家乳製品工場要等級 8。購買由伺服器決定；請求未完成或者存檔未能使用時，唔應顯示升級成功。
