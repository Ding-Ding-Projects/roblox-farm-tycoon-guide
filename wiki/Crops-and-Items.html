Crops and Items

The source defines seven planting routes: wheat, corn, carrot, soybean, sugarcane, strawberry and apple saplings. Each crop consumes one seed or sapling, has a level requirement and timer, and returns its listed yield plus XP and one planting supply if storage permits. Wheat has selected native harvest evidence. Other crop routes remain in development until their full earned and published paths are proved.

The item catalogue contains 31 named goods: seven planting supplies, seven harvested crops, four animal products, four feeds and nine made goods. Values on the live guide come from a source snapshot, not a promise that a public sale or order is currently available. Its search can locate every individual item and show how it is obtained and used.

The animals are chicken, cow, sheep and pig. Each requires its matching feed and has a collection timer and product. The chicken has selected local purchase, feeding and collection evidence; the others need full earned and live acceptance. See Buildings and Production.

農作物同物品

原始資料定義咗七條種植路線：小麥、粟米、紅蘿蔔、黃豆、甘蔗、士多啤梨同蘋果樹苗。每種農作物要用一粒種子或者一棵樹苗，亦有等級要求同計時器；倉庫有位先會收返所列產量、經驗值，同一份補充種植用品。小麥有選定嘅原生收成證據。其他農作物嘅完整賺取流程同公開版本未經證實，所以仍屬開發中。

物品圖鑑有 31 種有名貨品：七種種植用品、七種收成作物、四種動物產品、四種飼料，同九種加工貨品。網上指南顯示嘅價值嚟自一份原始資料快照，唔保證目前可以喺公開伺服器出售或者交訂單。搜尋功能可以搵到每件物品，睇佢點樣取得同使用。

動物有雞、牛、羊同豬。每種都要相應飼料，亦有收集計時器同產品。買雞、餵雞同收蛋已有選定嘅本機證據；其餘仍要完成賺取流程同公開版本驗收。詳情睇建築同生產。
