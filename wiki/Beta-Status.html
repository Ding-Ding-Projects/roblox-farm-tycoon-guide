Beta Status

The public Roblox beta is being prepared. A native Studio Save to File and independent reopen have source, root, service-property and Terrain-voxel parity evidence for the selected development scene. Selected local Play input reached plot Claim, Build, starter construction and some farm, factory, animal and amenity routes.

The beta is not called published or verified until a specific Roblox place version passes a fresh public join, responsive controls, durable leave and rejoin, and account-isolation acceptance. The 56 earned building and rotation routes and the concurrent 14-building arrangement also remain open. No play link appears on the website as a finished beta while that proof is absent.

The public development gallery contains reviewed captures. A gallery image proves only the scene and version recorded with it, not a live-server release.
